# Heard From the First Day

October 15, 2026 · Ruth Carpenter

## Theme

God hears our prayers from the first day. Delays are real, the enemy is real, and yet the enemy is limited, the battle is spiritual, and every prayer is kept and answered in God's timing.

**Key verse, Daniel 10:12 (KJV):**

> Then said he unto me, Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that thou didst set thine heart to understand, and to chasten thyself before thy God, thy words were heard, and I am come for thy words.

All scripture is King James Version. The study opens with Daniel 10 as the question and closes with Daniel 10 as the answer.

## 1. The Question: Daniel 10:2-13

Daniel prayed and fasted three weeks. Heaven answered on day one, and yet the answer took 21 days to arrive. Why?

> In those days I Daniel was mourning three full weeks. I ate no pleasant bread, neither came flesh nor wine in my mouth, neither did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled. (Daniel 10:2-3)

> But the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me one and twenty days: but, lo, Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me; and I remained there with the kings of Persia. (Daniel 10:13)

> Then said he, Knowest thou wherefore I come unto thee? and now will I return to fight with the prince of Persia: and when I am gone forth, lo, the prince of Grecia shall come. (Daniel 10:20)

**Notice:**

- The prayer was not stopped. It was **heard** the first day (v12)
- The **answer** was resisted in the spiritual realm (v13)
- Michael came to help. Heaven sends reinforcement
- Daniel kept praying and fasting the whole 21 days without knowing any of this

> And he said unto them, I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven. (Luke 10:18)

## 3. His Limits: Job 1-2 and Luke 22

Satan attacks, and yet he must ask permission and stay inside the boundary God sets.

> Hast not thou made an hedge about him, and about his house, and about all that he hath on every side? (Job 1:10)

> And the LORD said unto Satan, Behold, all that he hath is in thy power; only upon himself put not forth thine hand. (Job 1:12)

> And the LORD said unto Satan, Behold, he is in thine hand; but save his life. (Job 2:6)

**How the attack came:**

1. Sabeans took the oxen and asses and slew the servants (1:14-15)
2. Fire of God fell from heaven and burned the sheep and servants (1:16)
3. Chaldeans took the camels (1:17)
4. A great wind struck the house and killed his sons and daughters (1:18-19)
5. Sore boils from the sole of his foot unto his crown (2:7)
6. His wife: Curse God, and die (2:9)

The attacks came stacked, one messenger still speaking when the next arrived. The goal was for Job to curse God (1:11, 2:5).

**Job's response:**

> Naked came I out of my mother's womb, and naked shall I return thither: the LORD gave, and the LORD hath taken away; blessed be the name of the LORD. In all this Job sinned not, nor charged God foolishly. (Job 1:21-22)

**The restoration:**

> And the LORD turned the captivity of Job, when he prayed for his friends: also the LORD gave Job twice as much as he had before. (Job 42:10)

**The same pattern with Peter:**

> Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat: But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren. (Luke 22:31-32)

**Already defeated:**

> And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it. (Colossians 2:15)

> Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. (James 4:7)

## 4. The Real Battle: Ephesians 6:10-18

Paul explains what Daniel experienced. The fight is spiritual, and the last piece of the armour is prayer.

> Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places. (Ephesians 6:10-12)

> Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand. (Ephesians 6:13)

**The whole armour (6:14-17):**

- Loins girt about with truth
- Breastplate of righteousness
- Feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace
- Shield of faith, to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked
- Helmet of salvation
- Sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God

> Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints. (Ephesians 6:18)

**Connect it:** "Principalities" and "rulers of the darkness" in Ephesians 6:12 are the same kind of power as the prince of Persia in Daniel 10:13. Daniel's 21 days of prayer and fasting is Ephesians 6:18 in action.

## 5. The Delays

Scripture is full of people who waited. Each delay had a reason, and none of them were forgotten.

### Delay that requires persistence

**Elijah prays for rain (1 Kings 18:43-44, James 5:17-18)**

> And said to his servant, Go up now, look toward the sea. And he went up, and looked, and said, There is nothing. And he said, Go again seven times. And it came to pass at the seventh time, that he said, Behold, there ariseth a little cloud out of the sea, like a man's hand. (1 Kings 18:43-44)

**The persistent widow (Luke 18:1-8)**

> And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint. (Luke 18:1)

> And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them? I tell you that he will avenge them speedily. (Luke 18:7-8)

**The friend at midnight (Luke 11:5-10)**

> And I say unto you, Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you. (Luke 11:9)

**The Canaanite woman (Matthew 15:21-28)**

> But he answered her not a word. (Matthew 15:23)

> O woman, great is thy faith: be it unto thee even as thou wilt. And her daughter was made whole from that very hour. (Matthew 15:28)

### Delay for a bigger purpose

**Lazarus (John 11:1-44)**

> This sickness is not unto death, but for the glory of God, that the Son of God might be glorified thereby. (John 11:4)

> When he had heard therefore that he was sick, he abode two days still in the same place where he was. (John 11:6)

> Said I not unto thee, that, if thou wouldest believe, thou shouldest see the glory of God? (John 11:40)

Martha said "if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died" (11:21). Jesus wasn't late. He was waiting for something bigger than healing: resurrection.

**Jairus's daughter, twelve years old (Mark 5:21-43, Luke 8:40-56)**

Jairus begs Jesus to come. On the way, Jesus stops for the woman with an issue of blood twelve years (Mark 5:25). During that stop, word comes that the girl has died.

> Be not afraid, only believe. (Mark 5:36)

> And straightway the damsel arose, and walked; for she was of the age of twelve years. (Mark 5:42)

One woman waited twelve years, one girl had lived twelve years. Someone else's breakthrough delayed his, and yet both were made whole.

**The man born blind (John 9:1-7)**

> Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents: but that the works of God should be made manifest in him. (John 9:3)

The disciples asked if it was his sin or his parents'. Jesus said neither. Scripture doesn't record anyone praying for his sight, so use this one for the *purpose* behind a long affliction, not for delayed prayer.

### Long waits on a promise

**Abraham (Genesis 12:4, 21:5)**

Abram was seventy and five years old when he left Haran. Abraham was an hundred years old when Isaac was born. 25 years.

> Is any thing too hard for the LORD? At the time appointed I will return unto thee, according to the time of life, and Sarah shall have a son. (Genesis 18:14)

**Hannah (1 Samuel 1)**

> And she was in bitterness of soul, and prayed unto the LORD, and wept sore. (1 Samuel 1:10)

> For this child I prayed; and the LORD hath given me my petition which I asked of him. (1 Samuel 1:27)

**Zacharias and Elisabeth (Luke 1)**

KJV spells it Elisabeth.

> And they had no child, because that Elisabeth was barren, and they both were now well stricken in years. (Luke 1:7)

> Fear not, Zacharias: for thy prayer is heard; and thy wife Elisabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John. (Luke 1:13)

Same words as Daniel: "Fear not... thy prayer is heard." A prayer prayed years earlier, still on record.

**The man at the pool of Bethesda (John 5:1-9)**

> And a certain man was there, which had an infirmity thirty and eight years. When Jesus saw him lie, and knew that he had been now a long time in that case, he saith unto him, Wilt thou be made whole? (John 5:5-6)

> Jesus saith unto him, Rise, take up thy bed, and walk. (John 5:8)

38 years waiting for the water to move. Jesus knew how long he'd been there.

**Israel in Egypt (Exodus 2:23-24)**

> And God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob. (Exodus 2:24)

### Honest "how long?" prayers

> How long wilt thou forget me, O LORD? for ever? how long wilt thou hide thy face from me? (Psalm 13:1)

> I waited patiently for the LORD; and he inclined unto me, and heard my cry. He brought me up also out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings. (Psalm 40:1-2)

> O LORD, how long shall I cry, and thou wilt not hear! (Habakkuk 1:2)

> For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry. (Habakkuk 2:3)

### When the answer is different from the ask

**Paul's thorn (2 Corinthians 12:7-9)**

> And lest I should be exalted above measure through the abundance of the revelations, there was given to me a thorn in the flesh, the messenger of Satan to buffet me, lest I should be exalted above measure. For this thing I besought the Lord thrice, that it might depart from me. And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. (2 Corinthians 12:7-9)

The thorn is called a messenger of Satan, which ties straight back to Job: Satan afflicts, God limits it and uses it.

Scripture never says what the thorn was. Some teachers suggest Paul's eyesight, based on Galatians 4:15 ("ye would have plucked out your own eyes, and have given them to me") and Galatians 6:11 ("how large a letter I have written unto you with mine own hand"). That's a possibility, not a stated fact. Paul's three days of blindness at conversion were healed when scales fell from his eyes (Acts 9:9, 18).

## 6. Every Cry Is Kept: Revelation

The cry is raised in chapter 6 and answered in chapter 19. Nothing prayed is lost.

**The cry (fifth seal):**

> And when he had opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of them that were slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held: And they cried with a loud voice, saying, How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth? And white robes were given unto every one of them; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little season, until their fellowservants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they were, should be fulfilled. (Revelation 6:9-11)

**The prayers rise:**

> And another angel came and stood at the altar, having a golden censer; and there was given unto him much incense, that he should offer it with the prayers of all saints upon the golden altar which was before the throne. And the smoke of the incense, which came with the prayers of the saints, ascended up before God out of the angel's hand. And the angel took the censer, and filled it with fire of the altar, and cast it into the earth: and there were voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake. (Revelation 8:3-5)

**God answers:**

> Thou art righteous, O Lord, which art, and wast, and shalt be, because thou hast judged thus. For they have shed the blood of saints and prophets, and thou hast given them blood to drink; for they are worthy. (Revelation 16:5-6)

> For true and righteous are his judgments: for he hath judged the great whore, which did corrupt the earth with her fornication, and hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand. (Revelation 19:2)

**Connect it:** Luke 18:7-8 promised God would avenge his own elect which cry day and night. Revelation shows that promise kept.

> And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death. (Revelation 12:11)

## 7. Close: Back to Daniel

We started with "why the delay?" We end with "you were heard, you are loved, be strong while you wait."

> Then said he unto me, Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that thou didst set thine heart to understand, and to chasten thyself before thy God, thy words were heard, and I am come for thy words. (Daniel 10:12)

> And said, O man greatly beloved, fear not: peace be unto thee, be strong, yea, be strong. And when he had spoken unto me, I was strengthened, and said, Let my lord speak; for thou hast strengthened me. (Daniel 10:19)

> Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world. (1 John 4:4)

## Study Notes and Reflection

**Clarifications to keep the study accurate:**

- **Armageddon** is a place, not a name of Satan: "a place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon" (Revelation 16:16)
- **Lucifer** (Isaiah 14) and the **King of Tyrus** (Ezekiel 28) are addressed to human kings. Many read Satan behind them, especially in Ezekiel 28:13-15 ("Thou hast been in Eden the garden of God")
- **Prince of Persia** is a ruler in Satan's kingdom, not one of his names
- **Daniel's prayer** wasn't blocked. It was heard day one. The answer was resisted
- **Paul's eyesight** as the thorn is a possibility, not stated in scripture
- **The man born blind** and the **man at Bethesda** show long affliction and God's timing. Neither is recorded praying for healing

**Reflection questions:**

1. What have I been praying about that feels delayed? Have I stopped praying?
2. Where might I be fighting flesh and blood when the battle is spiritual?
3. Which piece of the armour am I neglecting?
4. Like Job, can I worship in the middle of loss, before the restoration?
5. Like Paul, can I receive "My grace is sufficient" if the answer is different from my ask?
6. What does it change to know my words were heard from the first day?
