## 1. The Question: Daniel 10:2-13

Point: Daniel prayed and fasted three weeks. Heaven answered on day one, and yet the answer took 21 days to arrive. Why?

> In those days I Daniel was mourning three full weeks. I ate no pleasant bread, neither came flesh nor wine in my mouth, neither did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled.
> [Daniel 10:2-3]

> Then said he unto me, Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that thou didst set thine heart to understand, and to chasten thyself before thy God, thy words were heard, and I am come for thy words.
> [Daniel 10:12]

**Notice:**

- The prayer was not stopped. It was **heard** the first day (v12)
- The **answer** was resisted in the spiritual realm (v13)
- Michael came to help. Heaven sends reinforcement
- Daniel kept praying and fasting the whole 21 days without knowing any of this

**How the attack came:**

2. Fire of God fell from heaven and burned the sheep and servants (1:16)

### Delay for a bigger purpose

**Lazarus (John 11:1-44)**

**Jairus's daughter, twelve years old (Mark 5:21-43, Luke 8:40-56)**

## Reflection

Clarifications:

- **Lucifer** (Isaiah 14) and the **King of Tyrus** (Ezekiel 28) are addressed to human kings. Many read Satan behind them, especially in Ezekiel 28:13-15
- **Prince of Persia** is a ruler in Satan's kingdom, not one of his names
- **Daniel's prayer** wasn't blocked. It was heard day one. The answer was resisted
- **Paul's eyesight** as the thorn is a possibility, not stated in scripture
- **The man born blind** and the **man at Bethesda** show long affliction and God's timing. Neither is recorded praying for healing

Questions:

1. What have I been praying about that feels delayed? Have I stopped praying?
2. Where might I be fighting flesh and blood when the battle is spiritual?
3. Which piece of the armour am I neglecting?
4. Like Job, can I worship in the middle of loss, before the restoration?
5. Like Paul, can I receive "My grace is sufficient" if the answer is different from my ask?
6. What does it change to know my words were heard from the first day?
